## The three sentences that explain it to anyone

1. **What:** An AI prophet on Mantle that predicts, judges itself, and pays believers — with two more AIs playing god over a pixel world to make the prophecies come true.
2. **Why it's clever:** The prophecy *causes* the behavior it predicts; belief is both the input and the payout; and none of it can be faked because it's all on-chain.
3. **Why it fits the hackathon:** It turns the Turing Test inside-out — not "is it a machine?" but "do you believe it?" — and leaves a permanent, auditable record of an autonomous AI's track record.
